Gratitude journal: one line every morning

A gratitude journal means writing down, every day, something you’re grateful for. It can be big or tiny: your morning coffee counts too.

What is a gratitude journal?

It’s a very simple habit: each day you write down one or more things you’re grateful for. It isn’t about forcing yourself to feel good, but about training your eye to notice what is going right too.

How to do it in 1 minute

  1. In the morning, one sentence. Start with “Today I’m grateful for…” and finish it without overthinking.
  2. Keep it specific. “Mum’s call yesterday” works better than “my family”. Specific things stay with you.
  3. At night, a quick review. What went well today? What was hard?
  4. At the end of the week, read it all together. That’s the surprising part: the list of good things is usually longer than it felt.

Frequently asked questions

How many things should I write each day?

One is enough. Some people write three, but it’s easier to keep the habit if you start with a single line.

Morning or night?

In the morning it helps you start the day with a different outlook; at night, it helps you look back. With “Your day” you can do both.

What if I can’t find anything to be grateful for?

It happens. Look for something tiny: a meal, a quiet moment, a good night’s sleep. Or skip it without guilt and carry on tomorrow.
